Water into Wine index

Monthly observations

What does the number mean?

An index of 120 means UK water supply prices have risen 20% relative to wine prices since January 2015. An index of 80 means they have fallen 20% relative to wine. It does not measure the cost of a litre of either product.

Methodology and sources

Calculated as 100 × (water CPI ÷ wine CPI) ÷ (the same ratio in January 2015), using monthly ONS CPI indices for water supply (D7DR) and wine (D7DH). The latest data month shown below is the latest observation in the published CSV, not necessarily the newest ONS release.
